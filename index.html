03 / 学术研究03 / Research

Research

学术研究

研究生阶段的行业研究报告，聚焦数字媒体转型与新闻教育。Postgraduate industry research report focusing on digital media transformation and journalism education.

Preparing the Next Generation of Journalists

Aligning Media Practice Education with Australia's Changing Digital Media Industry · 悉尼大学媒体实践硕士 · 1,649词Aligning Media Practice Education with Australia's Changing Digital Media Industry · Master of Media Practice, University of Sydney · 1,649 words

研究背景：Research Background: 随着人工智能与算法驱动的新闻分发在社交媒体平台的普及，传统新闻生产正在被重构。新闻生产——曾经依赖记者的写作内容——如今已成为数据统计、受众分析和平台分发的混合过程。澳大利亚政府数据显示，"记者与其他作家"行业正在整体收缩，年增长率预计为 -1.4%，而雇主对入门级岗位的要求已上升至平均 3.94 年工作经验。With the proliferation of AI and algorithm-driven news distribution on social media platforms, traditional journalism production is being restructured. News production — once reliant on journalists' writing — has become a hybrid process of data statistics, audience analysis, and platform distribution. Australian Government data shows the "Journalists and Other Writers" industry is contracting overall, with an annual growth rate projected at -1.4%, while employer requirements for entry-level positions have risen to an average of 3.94 years of work experience.

核心发现：Key Findings: 传统新闻编辑技能的价值正在下降，而对数据分析、SEO、受众洞察和多平台运营等混合技能的需求急剧上升。内容分发已从编辑判断转向算法策展，平台如今根据用户画像预测点击概率并据此排序内容。The value of traditional journalistic editing skills is declining, while demand for hybrid skills such as data analysis, SEO, audience insights, and multi-platform operations has surged. Content distribution has shifted from editorial judgment to algorithmic curation, with platforms now predicting click probability based on user profiles and ranking content accordingly.

三大建议：Three Key Recommendations: （1）增设AI与算法相关课程；（2）针对地理与性别支持，帮助国际学生建立就业社交网络；（3）加强正式行业导师项目。(1) Introduce AI and algorithm-related courses; (2) Address geographic and gender support to help international students build employment networking opportunities; (3) Strengthen formal industry mentorship programs.

完整摘要：Full Abstract: 传统线性新闻生产模式已被数据驱动的内容模式所取代。作为数字化趋势的一部分，未来从业者必须兼具创意叙事能力与科技技能。本报告提出的三大方案将为未来记者提供所需的技术能力。The traditional linear news production model has been replaced by data-driven content models. As part of the digitalization trend, future practitioners must combine creative storytelling with technological skills. The three recommendations proposed in this report will equip future journalists with needed technical capabilities.
